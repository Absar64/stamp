FAQ

Good questions

How many people can I connect?

As many as you like. Add a partner, your whole family, or the entire group chat — there's no limit on friends, and you can send to whoever you want from the app.

Does the other person need a Canvas too?

Nice to have, not required. Friends can receive on their own Canvas or right inside the free Stamp app — so you can start sending before their device even arrives.

Is there a subscription?

Never. Canvas is yours once you buy it — unlimited sending, no monthly fees, no paywalled features.

How fast does a letter arrive?

Under a second on Wi-Fi. The screen fades in your image and the LED ring glows softly so they notice without a buzzing phone.

What about privacy?

Letters are end-to-end encrypted and only visible to the people you've added. You can remove anyone from a Canvas at any time.

Returns and warranty?

30-day no-questions returns and a 1-year hardware warranty, backed by humans who actually answer.
