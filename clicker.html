FAQ

Good questions

What can a single click do?

Anything you map in the app — turn on lights, send a message, run a macro, switch a scene, or play a sound. Each of the three buttons can do something completely different.

Can it play custom sounds?

Yes. The built-in speaker can play anything you map it to, and you can upload your own sounds — use it as a soundboard even.

How long does the battery last?

Up to two weeks of typical use on a charge. Top it up over USB-C — no batteries to swap.

Does each button do something different?

It can. There are three independent buttons, and each supports single-press, double-press, and hold — so really you've got room for far more than three actions.

Do I need to know how to code?

Not at all. Everything is set up visually in the Stamp app in a couple of minutes, and you can re-map it whenever you like.

Returns and warranty?

30-day no-questions returns and a 1-year hardware warranty, backed by humans who actually answer.
